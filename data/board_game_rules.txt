Rules for "Star Traders" (a 2-4 player board game)

Setup:
Each player starts with 5 gold coins, 3 cargo ships, and 1 home planet card.
Shuffle the resource deck and deal 4 resource cards to each player.
Place the trade hub token in the center of the board.

Turn structure:
Each turn has three phases: Collect, Trade, and Build.
In the Collect phase, players gain 2 gold coins from their home planet.
In the Trade phase, players may exchange resource cards with the bank at a rate of 3 resources for 1 gold coin.
In the Build phase, players may spend gold coins to build outposts (cost: 4 gold coins each).

Winning the game:
A player wins immediately if they control 5 outposts.
If the resource deck runs out, the game ends and whoever has the most gold coins wins.
Players are eliminated if they run out of both gold coins and resource cards for two consecutive turns.

Special rule - Pirate Raids:
Every 5th turn, a Pirate Raid occurs. Each player must pay 1 gold coin or lose 1 cargo ship.
Players with zero cargo ships cannot participate in the Trade phase.
