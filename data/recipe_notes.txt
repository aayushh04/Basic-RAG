Grandma's Masala Chai Recipe (serves 2)

Ingredients:
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup whole milk
- 2 teaspoons loose black tea leaves (Assam CTC works best)
- 1 inch fresh ginger, crushed
- 2 green cardamom pods, crushed
- 1.5 tablespoons sugar (adjust to taste)

Steps:
1. Boil the water with crushed ginger and cardamom for 3 minutes.
2. Add the tea leaves and boil for another 2 minutes until the water turns dark reddish-brown.
3. Add the milk and sugar, then bring to a boil.
4. Once it rises to the top, reduce heat and simmer for 1 minute.
5. Strain into cups and serve hot.

Tips:
- For a stronger chai, boil the tea leaves for an extra minute before adding milk.
- Adding a pinch of black pepper helps during cold or flu.
- Store unused ginger in the freezer to keep it fresh longer.
- Never let the chai boil over — reduce heat as soon as it rises.
