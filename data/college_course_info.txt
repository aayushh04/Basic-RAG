Course Handbook Excerpt: CS301 - Data Structures and Algorithms

Instructor: Dr. Priya Nair
Credits: 4
Semester: Fall

Grading breakdown:
- Midterm exam: 25%
- Final exam: 35%
- Assignments (4 total): 20%
- Lab work: 15%
- Class participation: 5%

Attendance policy:
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to be eligible for the final exam.
Two late arrivals count as one absence.
Medical leave requires a certificate submitted within 7 days.

Assignment submission rules:
Assignments are submitted via the course portal before 11:59 PM on the due date.
Late submissions lose 10% of the assignment's marks per day, up to 3 days late.
After 3 days, the assignment receives a zero unless an extension was approved in advance.

Office hours:
Dr. Nair holds office hours every Tuesday and Thursday, 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM, in Room 214.
Teaching assistants hold additional help sessions on Mondays, 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM, in the CS lab.
