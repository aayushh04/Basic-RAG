TRAINING RULES
• Compounds: 1–2 reps in reserve
• Isolation: 0–2 reps in reserve
• Heavy compounds rest: 2–3 min
• Isolation rest: 60–90 sec
• Focus on progressive overload
• When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets → increase weight
• Prioritize good form and controlled reps
• Warm up before heavy compound exercises
